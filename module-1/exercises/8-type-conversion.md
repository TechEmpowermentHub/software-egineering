# 8. Type Conversion

**Goal:** Understand how programs represent different types of data.

Write a program that asks the user for their age and then calculates what their age will be in 10 years.

**Example**

Enter your age: 20

In 10 years, you will be 30.

Your program should convert the user's input into a number before performing the calculation.
